Manifesto for AI-Age Development

The Agile Manifesto (2001) was written for human teams. Demerzel operates where AI agents build software alongside humans. This demands new principles.

1
Governance over heroics — Agents need constitutional guardrails, not just talented operators.
2
Compounding over sprinting — Every cycle should leave the system stronger. Measure Dc > 1.0, not velocity.
3
Bounded autonomy over full delegation — Trust is earned through calibrated confidence: ≥0.9 proceed, ≥0.7 note, ≥0.5 confirm, <0.3 stop.
4
Tetravalent truth over binary status — Unknown triggers investigation. Contradictory triggers escalation. Admitting uncertainty is strength.
5
Observable conscience over hidden judgment — Track regrets, detect patterns, learn visibly.
6
Reactive governance over periodic review — Watch → detect → act → compound in real time.
7
Constitutional hierarchy over flat rules — Zeroth Law overrides everything. Not all rules are equal.
8
Completeness instinct over gap tolerance — Proactively ask: what's missing? What's the dual? What breaks at scale? (VSM System 4)
9
Factory of factories over manual creation — Don't create things; create systems that create things.
10
Human-AI collaboration over human-or-AI — Neither pure automation nor pure control. The human is always in the loop — the question is where.
